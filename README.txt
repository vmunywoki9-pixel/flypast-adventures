HOW TO ADD YOUR LOGO
1. Put your logo image in this same folder, beside index.html.
2. Rename the file exactly: fleypat-logo.png
3. The header and footer on every page will display it automatically.
4. If your logo is JPG or WEBP, either convert it to PNG or update the src="fleypat-logo.png" references in the HTML files to the exact filename.

HOW THE ENQUIRIES WORK
- The homepage trip finder opens WhatsApp with the selected trip details.
- The contact form opens the visitor's default email application with the inquiry pre-filled.
- The contact form is not connected to a server. To receive submissions without relying on the visitor's email application, connect a form service or backend before launch.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Confirm all package prices, inclusions, availability and applicable park/entry fees.
- Replace sample testimonials with genuine reviews used with customer permission.
- Confirm business details, office hours, service availability and any required licences.
- Test every page and contact link on mobile and desktop.
- The website uses remotely hosted Unsplash photographs; an internet connection is required for those images to load.

To preview, open index.html in a web browser. Upload all files together to the same website directory on your hosting account.
