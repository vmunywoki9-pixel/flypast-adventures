Travel Tips & Safari Guides

Helpful inspiration and practical pointers as you plan your next Kenya or East African adventure.

Safari planning

What to Pack for Your First Kenyan Safari

Choose comfortable neutral clothing, sun protection, suitable footwear, personal medication and a camera or binoculars if you have them. Check your itinerary for weather and activity-specific needs.

Coastal travel

Planning a Beach Holiday on Kenya's Coast

Consider the season, sea conditions, preferred activities, accommodation location and transfer times. Build flexibility into your plans for a more relaxed coastal stay.

Destination guide

Exploring Tsavo East & West

Think about the time available, park gates, driving distances, accommodation and the experiences you want to include when planning a Tsavo safari.
