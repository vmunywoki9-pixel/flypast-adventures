Who we are

Travel is More Than Reaching a Destination

Fleypat Adventures is a tour and travel company established on 9 April 2022 and headquartered in Mombasa, Kenya. Founded by Felix Ivayo, the company grew from a passion for travel, exploration and creating meaningful experiences.

We connect travellers with exciting destinations and experiences across Kenya and international destinations. Our services include wildlife safaris, beach holidays, excursions, hotel reservations, airport transfers, group and corporate tours, school trips, air ticketing, car hire and transportation facilitation.

We understand that every traveller has different interests, expectations and budgets. Our approach is centered on listening to those needs and developing suitable travel arrangements.
